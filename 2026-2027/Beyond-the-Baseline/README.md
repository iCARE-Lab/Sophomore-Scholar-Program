## Main Research Question

> **How effectively can Vision-Language Models (such as CLIP) classify cancer images across zero-shot, few-shot (10-shot), and full-data settings compared to traditional ResNet50 baselines?**

### Research Workflow

`Histopathology & Dermatoscopy Data` → `Zero-Shot / 10-Shot / Full-Data Setup` → `VLM (CLIP) vs. ResNet50` → `Linear Probing & Fine-Tuning` → `Evaluation (Accuracy, F1, AUC-ROC)` → `Error Analysis` → `Manuscript & Symposium`
